# ZINC positioning

Working reference for copy and strategy on wearezinc.com. Update it as the
strategy evolves; site copy should trace back to something here.

## Agency

ZINC helps clients choose, build, and evolve the right digital foundation for
their business, using AI where it adds value. It is an experienced strategy,
brand, design, and technology agency. AI-native websites and enterprise CMS
are both credible choices, chosen by business need.

Four solution categories: Websites, E-commerce Acceleration, AI Enablement,
Strategy & Brand.

## Brand line and purpose (updated Sept 23, 2026)

**Brand line: See Further, Go Bigger.** Title case with a comma. In visuals,
it is bracketed by outward chevrons: `< See Further,  Go Bigger >`.

**Purpose:** Bring ambitious companies closer to their customers, with the
clarity to see what's next and a partner to build it.

The original pillars predate the AI-native work. This version balances three
things:

- **The purpose leads.** ZINC brings companies closer to their customers. AI
  website migration and Dispatch are proof of how we deliver that today, not
  the whole story.
- **Control is a choice, not a handoff.** Some clients want to steer
  themselves. Many still want a partner for support and execution. Don't
  frame ZINC as handing over the keys and leaving.
- **The client is the hero, ZINC is the guide.** Steering and helm language
  is welcome. Sound bite: "You're at the helm. We're right beside you."

**SEE FURTHER: Clarity. Insight. Foundation.** Understanding. ZINC as the
guide in a fast-changing AI landscape.

- Clarity. Cut through noise and see what actually drives outcomes.
- Insight. Understand your customers, and how AI is changing the ways they
  find you, choose, and buy.
- Foundation. Choose the right platform by business need: AI-native or
  enterprise CMS.
- Positioning. Build brands and narratives that stand apart in an
  AI-saturated market.
- Alignment. Bring leadership, marketing, and technology into one vision.
- Foresight. Anticipate how markets, customers, and technology will evolve.

**GO BIGGER: Execution. Control. Scale.** Advancement. Turning clarity into
momentum, together.

- Execution. Design, build, and launch with precision and speed, alongside
  the client's team.
- Control. The client steers as much as they want. On AI-native sites,
  Dispatch previews changes, routes approvals, and keeps an attributed
  history with restore.
- Scale. Systems and workflows that grow with demand.
- Speed. On AI-native sites, teams change what they need by asking.
- Partnership. Strategy, brand, design, and new builds, for as long as the
  client needs them.
- Growth. Translate strategic vision into tangible business results.

**Ways to work.** The client picks how hands-on to be and can change it over
time:

- **Hands-on.** The client's team makes changes by asking, governed by
  Dispatch.
- **Side by side.** The client's team handles day-to-day updates, and ZINC
  leads strategy, campaigns, and new builds.
- **Fully supported.** ZINC plans, builds, and runs it with them.

**The mark.** The Z is two chevrons. The top `>` is the company side and the
bottom `<` is the customer side, and they meet to form the Z. ZINC brings the
two sides together so they work as one. The mark also maps to "choose, build,
and evolve":

- `>` See Further is choose. ZINC guides.
- The Z is build. ZINC and the client, side by side.
- `<` Go Bigger is evolve. The client steers, with as much ZINC as they want.

Outward chevrons mean opening up and expanding, not separation.

## AI-native websites and Dispatch

**The one-line offer: AI website migration, with control built in.**
(Added Sept 22, 2026.)

What it means:

- **AI website migration.** ZINC moves an existing site onto a foundation the
  client's team can update by asking an AI agent, keeping the design and
  content they value.
- **With control built in.** The control is Dispatch, ZINC's governance
  platform, configured as part of the migration rather than bolted on after:
  changes are previewed before release, approvals follow the client's
  workflow, there is a record of who asked for and approved what, and there is
  a tested recovery process for supported changes.

Where to use it:

- As the short answer to "what does ZINC offer for AI-native websites?" in
  sales conversations, social, and introductions.
- As a candidate headline, subhead, or summary line on the AI-native and
  AI Website Migration pages, the homepage website feature, and Dispatch
  mentions.

Guardrails:

- Introduce Dispatch on first use as "our governance platform, Dispatch."
- "Control" means configured review, approval, history, and recovery. Do not
  imply unconditional safety or universal undo; restoring code does not
  reverse form submissions, emails, or changes in external systems.
- Do not apply the line to Enterprise Websites & CMS. There, Dispatch is
  optional and assessed per workflow.
- No em dashes in site copy.

## Dispatch (updated Sept 22, 2026)

**Definition:** Dispatch is the management layer for AI-powered websites.
Your agent builds. Your team approves. Dispatch keeps every change visible,
attributable, reviewable, and reversible.

**Bridge sentence:** ZINC helps you choose and build the right website.
Dispatch helps your team and its AI agents run it without losing control.

**Full-site bridge:** For more than 25 years, ZINC has helped companies make
better business, brand, creative, and technology decisions. Today that
includes a new question: how should your website work when AI agents can
build and update it? We help you choose the right path, deliver the website,
and put Dispatch around it so your team can move faster with control.

Hierarchy: ZINC is the agency. AI-native websites and migration are the
growth wedge. Dispatch is ZINC's proprietary control advantage. AEO is an
accelerator, not the lead.

Preferred language: management layer for AI-powered websites, AI website
governance, agent-ready stack, bring your own agent (BYOA), preview before
release, roles and risk tiers, attributed change history, one-click restore,
improve / migrate / redesign.

Never lead with: prompt vault or library, AI asset vault, image library,
tool library, Notion vault, "single source of truth for anything AI," or an
AI operating system for the whole company.

Claims discipline: "restore," not "nothing can go wrong"; "preserve URLs
where possible," not "zero SEO impact"; "compatible site," not "works with
every website"; "agents such as," not an exhaustive list; "AEO signals and
monitoring," not guaranteed AI-search rankings. WordPress can be named as the
main migration opportunity; handle Webflow neutrally, case by case.

Related page promises: "Your move to AI-native. Handled by ZINC." (migration
page) and "Keep the website you love. Change how you run it." (AI-native page).
