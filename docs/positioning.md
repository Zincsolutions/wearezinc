# ZINC positioning

Working reference for copy and strategy on wearezinc.com. Update it as the
strategy evolves; site copy should trace back to something here.

## Agency

ZINC helps clients choose, build, and evolve the right digital foundation for
their business, using AI where it adds value. It is an experienced strategy,
brand, design, and technology agency. AI-native websites and enterprise CMS
are both credible choices, chosen by business need.

Four solution categories: Websites, E-commerce Acceleration, AI Enablement,
Strategy & Brand.

## AI-native websites and Dispatch

**The one-line offer: AI website migration, with control built in.**
(Added Sept 22, 2026.)

What it means:

- **AI website migration.** ZINC moves an existing site onto a foundation the
  client's team can update by asking an AI agent, keeping the design and
  content they value.
- **With control built in.** The control is Dispatch, ZINC's governance
  platform, configured as part of the migration rather than bolted on after:
  changes are previewed before release, approvals follow the client's
  workflow, there is a record of who asked for and approved what, and there is
  a tested recovery process for supported changes.

Where to use it:

- As the short answer to "what does ZINC offer for AI-native websites?" in
  sales conversations, social, and introductions.
- As a candidate headline, subhead, or summary line on the AI-native and
  AI Website Migration pages, the homepage website feature, and Dispatch
  mentions.

Guardrails:

- Introduce Dispatch on first use as "our governance platform, Dispatch."
- "Control" means configured review, approval, history, and recovery. Do not
  imply unconditional safety or universal undo; restoring code does not
  reverse form submissions, emails, or changes in external systems.
- Do not apply the line to Enterprise Websites & CMS. There, Dispatch is
  optional and assessed per workflow.
- No em dashes in site copy.
