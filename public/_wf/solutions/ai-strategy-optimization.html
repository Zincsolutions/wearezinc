What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of optimizing your website and content so it appears directly in AI-generated answers from platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google’s AI experiences.Rather than competing for links or rankings, AEO focuses on making your content clear, authoritative, and structured so answer engines can confidently cite it when users ask questions in natural language.AEO combines technical foundations (structured data and schema), answer-ready content strategy, and ongoing measurement of visibility and referrals from AI-driven discovery.

How is AEO different from traditional SEO?

SEO optimizes for rankings. AEO optimizes for being the answer.Traditional SEO focuses on keyword-based searches and search engine results pages. AEO focuses on conversational prompts and inclusion within AI-generated responses.

Key differences include:

  • Query type: keywords vs. natural-language questions.
  • Objective: rankings vs. citations and recommendations
  • Content structure: blog-first vs. answer-ready formats
  • Technical depth: basic on-page SEO vs. structured data and schema
  • Measurement: search rankings vs. AI visibility and referral tracking

AEO and SEO work together. Content optimized for answer engines also performs better as Google continues shifting toward AI-powered search experiences.

Who benefits most from AEO?

AEO is critical for B2B organizations whose buyers use AI tools to research solutions before engaging with sales.

This includes:

  • B2B SaaS and technology companies
  • Professional services and consultancies
  • Manufacturing and industrial businesses
  • Technical depth: basic on-page SEO vs. structured data and schema
  • Fintech, cybersecurity, and regulated industries

AEO is especially valuable for companies with longer sales cycles, competitive markets, and existing investments in content and SEO.

If buyers are asking answer engines for recommendations in your category and you’re not included you’re invisible at the moment of discovery.

How do you measure AEO success?

We measure AEO performance across three levels:

Answer Engine Visibility

  • Presence and frequency in AI-generated answers
  • Citation position and context
  • Competitive coverage across priority prompts
  • Sentiment and framing of mentions

Traffic & Engagement

  • Referral traffic from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google’s AI experiences
  • Conversion rates from answer-engine visitors
  • Engagement metrics and assisted attribution

Pipeline Impact

  • MQLs and SQLs influenced by AEO visibility
  • Opportunity creation and pipeline value
  • Revenue attribution tied to AI-assisted discovery

Reporting is delivered monthly and used to continuously refine strategy.
