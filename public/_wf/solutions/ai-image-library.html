FAQs

Find answers to your questions about our AI Dispatch platform and implementation processes.

What’s included in Dispatch?

Dispatch is a comprehensive AI asset management platform built for modern teams. Included in every Dispatch Vault are:

A centralized strategy hub to house your AI roadmap

Organized prompt and workflow libraries

A brand-aligned AI image library

Approved templates, tools, and automation guides

Documentation for AI-powered workflows and processes
Dispatch provides everything you need to unify, scale, and manage your company’s AI adoption—all in one place.

How quickly can we get set up?

You can launch your AI Dispatch system in as little as two weeks. Our onboarding process includes a focused workshop to assess your current AI tools, processes, and goals, followed by custom setup and integration of your assets. Most teams are up and running with a tailored AI asset vault, workflow templates, and prompt libraries within 10–15 business days.

Can Dispatch grow with our team?

Absolutely. Dispatch is designed for scalable AI workflow automation and knowledge management. As your organization adopts new AI tools or expands to new teams, Dispatch evolves with you—adding categories, permissions, and features as needed. It’s the flexible foundation for long-term AI strategy and growth.

Is it tool-agnostic?

Yes. Dispatch is a tool-agnostic AI asset management solution. It works seamlessly whether your team uses GPT-4, Midjourney, Jasper, Copy.ai, or any other AI tool. Your prompts, images, workflows, and documentation are organized by use case—not by vendor—so you’re always ready to adapt as new AI models and technologies emerge.

Do you provide training and support?

Yes—our AI enablement experts provide comprehensive onboarding, hands-on training, and ongoing support. We help your team integrate Dispatch into daily operations, optimize your AI workflows, and stay ahead of new developments in artificial intelligence. Plus, your Dispatch Vault is continuously updated with best practices and new AI solutions as they become available.
