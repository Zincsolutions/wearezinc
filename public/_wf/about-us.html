FAQs

Here are some common questions about our services and how we can help you.

How long has ZINC been in business?

ZINC has been a thriving digital agency for over 25 years, founded in 1998. We’ve delivered hundreds of successful projects for companies ranging from Fortune 100 leaders to fast-growing startups.

What makes ZINC different from other digital agencies?

We combine decades of proven digital strategy and design expertise with the latest in AI-powered tools and workflows. This allows us to help brands move faster, scale smarter, and optimize for both traditional search engines and AI-generated search results.

What types of companies does Zinc work with?

Zinc partners with companies ranging from Fortune 100 corporations to early-stage startups across multiple industries. Our clients include healthcare organizations, fintech companies, e-commerce brands, B2B technology firms, and consumer product companies. We've successfully delivered projects in healthcare, financial services, technology, retail, manufacturing, and professional services. Our industry-agnostic approach means we bring cross-sector insights to every project.

What platforms and technologies does Zinc build websites on?

Zinc builds websites on leading platforms including Webflow, Shopify, WordPress, and custom solutions depending on client needs. We specialize in creating sites optimized for AI search, mobile performance, and scalability. Our technical expertise includes responsive design, e-commerce integration, CMS implementation, API development, and third-party integrations. All our websites are built with AI search optimization, fast loading speeds, and mobile-first design principles.

What services does ZINC offer?

Our core services include AI Enablement & Strategy, AI Search Optimization (AEO/GEO), Web Design & Branding, E-commerce Solutions, Technology & Platform Integration, and AI-powered Content Systems.
